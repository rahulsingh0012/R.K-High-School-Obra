ABOUT OUR INSTITUTION

R.K. High School, Obra

R.K. High School, Obra is a premier state government co-educational secondary and higher secondary institution situated in Obra, Aurangabad, Bihar. Operating under the administrative guidance of the Department of Education, Government of Bihar, the school prepares scholars for comprehensive academic excellence.

Equipped with standardized science laboratories, modern information technology infrastructure, dedicated vocational education wings, and an active sports ecosystem, the institution remains dedicated to holistic student progression.

🏫 Government School 📚 BSEB Affiliated 💼 NSQF Vocational Hub 🌱 Holistic Learning
🎓

Institutional Vision

To nurture academically proficient, morally conscious, and practically skilled citizens equipped to make positive contributions to society.

Our Mission

Delivering high-standard secondary education along with vocational training to equip learners for technical careers and higher academic pursuits.
