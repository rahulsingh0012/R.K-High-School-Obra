ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

R.K. High School, Obra

R.K. High School, Obra is committed to providing students with a supportive environment for academic learning, discipline, character building and all-round development.

Established in 1945, R.K. High School, Obra is a premier state-managed co-educational institution located in the Obra block of the Aurangabad district in Bihar. Operating under the Department of Education, Bihar, the school serves students from Grades 9 through 12, preparing them for higher academic success under the curriculum framework of the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB).
